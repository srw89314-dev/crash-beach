# Crash Beach — itch.io page draft

## Short description

Survive the first three days after a plane crash, build a routine, and discover what the island expects from you.

## Page copy

Crash Beach is a desktop browser survival game about turning wreckage into a foothold. Gather supplies, find fresh water, craft what you need, repel predators, and decide how much you trust the island—and the other survivor who washed up here.

Version 0.50 is a focused three-day vertical slice with nine journal objectives and a clear completion moment. Finish the slice, then keep exploring the larger open-ended survival prototype.

### Features

- A guided three-day survival arc with persistent journal progress
- Resource gathering, crafting, hunger, thirst, a player-built shelter, and changing weather
- Turn-based predator encounters with a skill-timed dodge/block meter
- Solo and Duo modes, adjustable difficulty, tone options, and Continue or Permadeath saves
- Procedural music and sound that react to safety and danger
- Local browser saves; no account, analytics, or online connection required after loading

### Controls

Move with `WASD` or the arrow keys. Press `E` to interact, `C` to craft, `H` to hunt, `K` to save at camp in Continue mode, `Space` for the battle timing meter, and `Escape` for options.
